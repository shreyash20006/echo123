About Us

Echo.TGPCOP | Campus Media is the official media team of our campus. From fests and seminars to sports days and cultural nights, we capture it all through photos, reels and creative design, so every memory lives on.

Behind the lens, in the edit room, on the stage: this is a team of students who love telling campus stories.
