Official Campus Media Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Echo.TGPCOP Campus Media & Creative Cell

Welcome to Echo.TGPCOP (accessible at echotgpcopmedia.site), the official student media and creative cell of TGPCOP. We value the privacy of our students, faculty, and campus community. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect your personal data when you use our website and Google Sign-In services.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, we collect information in the following ways:

  • Google Authentication Information: When you log in with Google, we receive your basic public profile details authorized by you, including your name, email address, and profile avatar. This is handled securely through Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Student Submissions & Content: If you upload performances, campus coverage, photography, or reels via our Student Showcase, we collect your submitted name, category, title, video or photo files, and optional Instagram handle.
  • Technical & Usage Data: We may process standard device and browser headers for performance optimization, responsive rendering, and session security.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to provide, maintain, and improve our campus media platform, specifically:

  • To authenticate students and verify eligibility to post media.
  • To properly attribute credit (student creator name) to uploaded creative performances, reels, and photos.
  • To grant uploaders authorized access to manage or delete their own submissions at any time.
  • To communicate important campus event coverage and media opportunities.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize student personal information under any circumstances. We integrate only with reputable industry-standard infrastructure:

  • Google OAuth 2.0: Used strictly for secure student sign-in authentication.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud PostgreSQL database and media storage protected by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Only authenticated owners can delete their uploaded files.

4. User Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We respect your ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Delete Your Content: When logged in, students can delete any of their uploaded videos or photos at any time directly on the Showcase page using the 🗑️ Delete button.
  • Complete Account Deletion: If you wish to have your account record or all associated media permanently purged from our database, email us at Media@tgpcop.site with your request, and your data will be removed within 48 hours.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize local browser storage strictly to preserve your active authentication session and remember your theme preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform enhancements or college guidelines. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
