# Echo.TGPCOP — Campus Media Cell Website

Official, standalone, and responsive web portal for **Echo.TGPCOP** (The Campus Media & Creative Cell of **Tulsiramji Gaikwad-Patil College of Pharmacy, Nagpur**).

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Brand Identity & Aesthetics**
   - High-energy electric palette: Electric Blue (`#004BFE`), Radiant Yellow (`#FFD60A`), Deep Navy (`#002DAA`), and clean White (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Clean custom geometric SVG logo mark with aperture icon & crisp typography.
   - Distinctive typography using Google Fonts: *Bebas Neue*, *Bowlby One*, and *Poppins*.

2. **Top Live Campus Marquee**
   - Infinite smooth ticker broadcasting real-time media drops, audition notices, and fest highlights.

3. **Hero Section with 3D Floating Collage**
   - Live pulse badge: `OFFICIAL MEDIA & CREATIVE CELL • TGPCOP`.
   - Floating polaroid photo frames with subtle continuous floating animations & fun graphic stickers (`100% RAW VIBES`, `4K 60FPS`).
   - Interactive camera photo snap demo button.

4. **Web Audio API Camera Shutter Sound & Visual Flash**
   - Synthesized authentic camera shutter click sound (no external MP3 downloads required, works offline & CORS-free).
   - Instant visual screen flash effect simulating a camera flash.
   - Shutter sound toggle button in the navbar (`🔊` / `🔇`).

5. **About & Live Campus Statistics**
   - Mission statement documenting pharmacy college life, National Pharmacy Week (NPW), cultural events, and research symposiums.
   - Interactive animated number counters:
     - 58+ Campus Events Covered
     - 180+ Reels & Cinematic Recaps
     - 36+ Student Creators
     - 100% Pure Campus Energy

6. **Four Creative Pillars (Interactive 3D Tilt Cards)**
   - Still Photography (Stage & candids)
   - Reels & Aftermovies (Viral cuts & color grading)
   - Creatives & Branding (Posters, brochures, merchandise)
   - PR & Vox-Pops (Student interviews & red carpet)

7. **Visual Archive / Interactive Gallery**
   - Category filtering: `All Moments`, `Fests & Culture`, `Pharmacy Week`, `Campus Life`, `Portraits`.
   - Interactive like buttons with instant feedback.
   - Fullscreen Lightbox modal with keyboard navigation (Esc, Left/Right arrow keys) and next/prev buttons.

8. **Featured Reels Showcase**
   - Vertical Instagram-style reel previews with viewer stats and play previews.

9. **Behind The Lens (The Gear Locker)**
   - Showcase of cinema tools: Sony Alpha bodies, G-Master primes, DJI Gimbals, Rode wireless audio systems.

10. **The Core Squad (Team Spotlight)**
    - Polaroid member cards with roles, academic branches, and social handles.

11. **Interactive Campus Memory Wall**
    - Live student shoutout board where visitors can pin messages and praise, rendered instantly.

12. **Join The Squad / Audition Form**
    - Role selector pills (`Photography`, `Reel Editing`, `Graphic Design`, `Anchoring & PR`, `Scriptwriting`, `Social Media`).
    - Form fields with live client-side validation and persistent local storage saving.

13. **Responsive & Mobile-Ready**
    - Fully optimized for desktop, tablets, and smartphones with mobile drawer navigation.
